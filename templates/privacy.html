Privacy

  • AFM stores your account email, uploaded resumes, pasted job descriptions, generated resumes, applications and their statuses — nothing else.
  • Resume and JD text are sent to the configured LLM provider (Anthropic Claude API) only when you press "Generate tailored resume".
  • Resume text, JD text and email bodies are never written to logs.
  • Uploads and generated PDFs are streamed only to the signed-in owner through login-protected views.
  • Limits: uploads are PDF/DOCX up to 5 MB; sending is capped at 20 emails per user per day.
  • "Delete my account and data" removes your account, uploaded files, analyses, generated PDFs and applications.
